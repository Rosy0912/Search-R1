## Version-specific finding

The exact official21.08.5 release [slurmstepd manual](https://github.com/SchedMD/slurm/blob/slurm-21-08-5-1/doc/man/man8/slurmstepd.8) lists SIGTERM among ignored signals. Its [slurmstepd.c](https://github.com/SchedMD/slurm/blob/slurm-21-08-5-1/src/slurmd/slurmstepd/slurmstepd.c) blocks that signal at startup. The matching [mgr.c](https://github.com/SchedMD/slurm/blob/slurm-21-08-5-1/src/slurmd/slurmstepd/mgr.c) handles SIGTERM in a special X11 helper rather than a general step-daemon graceful-shutdown handler. This supports an inference that sending TERM to this ordinary old stepdaemon will not complete its cleanup; actual locally installed executable/manual/build must confirm whether a site patch changes that behavior. The [current online manual](https://slurm.schedmd.com/slurmstepd.html) now describes clean shutdown on TERM and is insufficient evidence for this21.08 node.

Slurm completion normally includes task termination, epilog and a completion RPC to the controller; a disappeared process alone does not establish node readiness. See the official [job termination design](https://slurm.schedmd.com/job_launch.html). The [troubleshooting guide](https://slurm.schedmd.com/troubleshoot.html) identifies hung I/O/nonkillable tasks and controller/daemon communication among completing-state concerns. Its node reset/reboot remedies are administrator operations beyond the job3315-only stop scope.
